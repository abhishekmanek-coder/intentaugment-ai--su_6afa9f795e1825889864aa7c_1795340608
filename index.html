Pipeline Architecture & Data Flow

End-to-end operational flow through the four core transformation stages.

📥
1. Raw Ingestion
Ingests support chat & call transcripts across 8 balanced business domains.
⚡
2. Augment Engine
Parallel transformer paraphrasing, QWERTY typos, OCR slips, and contractions.
🛡️
3. Semantic Gate
Cosine similarity filter [0.80, 0.98] discards semantic drift & trivial identity copies.
📦
4. Model Packaging
Generates zero-leakage splits for training production-grade intent classifiers.
